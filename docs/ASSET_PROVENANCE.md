# Asset Provenance Ledger

This ledger records provenance evidence; it is **not blanket legal or release clearance**.
Owner-supplied files are not necessarily owner-authored. A file header, Git author, import
manifest, successful export or visual approval cannot establish commercial or redistribution
rights. `COPYRIGHT.md` reserves the project's original rights; it does not grant rights in
unknown or third-party inputs.

**Current dialogue portraits (#308):** The [record of the game author's confirmation](https://github.com/BrainEno/where-is-school/issues/308#issuecomment-7038464634)
states that the author hand-drew the protagonist, Q and N, then used ChatGPT to process those
hand drawings into the portraits used in the game. This explicitly covers [the current Q redraw `35b4560`](https://github.com/BrainEno/where-is-school/commit/35b4560039914b0a57eec2666a3743b5138bb8da);
it does not carry the #64 statement over from the old Q portrait. The `art/portraits/` paths
below identify the supplied game assets, not where the original hand drawings are stored.

This records the artist and the service used, not clearance of the output. The processing
dates, model versions and applicable terms are unverified; commercial use and redistribution
of the current portraits remain **UNKNOWN / NOT CLEARED** for public or store release.
Closing #308 after the attribution is recorded does not change that status.

**Issue #64 status:** [The owner's #64 confirmation](https://github.com/BrainEno/where-is-school/issues/64#issuecomment-6941291502)
records that the player GLB was made by the repository owner in Blender with Python.
Q's approved 3D model and the 16 environment models retain their separate confirmations.
The Universal Animation Library (UAL) and its bake are retired from the current tree by
owner decision. The project still prohibits shipping or reintroducing them; removal is
not a licence decision for Git history, old CI artifacts or existing clones.

See [the bounded character/portrait/animation audit](ASSET_LICENSE_AUDIT.md) for the per-asset
rights table, exact hashes and source-to-runtime links, public licence evidence, verifier
regressions, validation limits and the questions only the Owner can answer. Other entries
below retain their existing provenance records; this review does not certify every asset in
the repository. Material not imported is listed separately at the end.

## Notes

- The laboratory scene and its materials are build artefacts of
  `tools/build_laboratory.py`, and its four textures are build artefacts of
  `art/laboratory/paint_laboratory_textures.py`; both are checked byte for byte in CI, so
  the scripts are the provenance of both. `art/laboratory/README.md` records what each
  field is, why it tiles and why it is quiet.
- The audio files are regenerated deterministically by
  `python3 tools/generate_placeholder_audio.py`; the script is the provenance.
  `WIS_AUDIO_OUT` redirects the write, which is how `tests/bell_sound_test.py` proves the
  shipped bell is byte-identical to what the script produces today.
- **There is no dialogue audio.** The repository once carried nine clips under
  `assets/audio/voice/`, total length 1.8 s, against 228 spoken lines, and every one of them
  was between 0.05 s and 0.28 s - a fragment, not a line. A tenth clip covered an inspection
  whose `text` and `repeat_text` differ in length by a factor of three, so no single recording
  could fit both. They have been removed rather than left as decoys that make the voice system
  look wired when it is not. Dialogue is text, paced by the player; `tests/voice_coverage_test.py`
  fails if a clip is ever added that does not match the line it claims to speak.
- The four interaction cue sounds are not files at all: `scripts/audio/cue_audio.gd`
  synthesises them at startup, in the same additive style, from constants in that script. The
  script is therefore the whole provenance, and there is nothing to import, license or lose.
- The bundled fonts are the two Noto faces above. Noto Sans SC is needed
  because Godot's built-in font has no CJK coverage, so simplified Chinese text cannot
  render without it. Noto Serif SC is the dialogue panel's reading face (owner's direction,
  2026-11-12), split and checked the same way by the same script; its licence is
  `assets/fonts/NotoSerifSC-OFL.txt`. The license text travels with the file (`assets/fonts/OFL.txt`).
  The full font is a source file under `art/` (`.gdignore`d, never imported); the
  game loads a subset built from the project's own text by `tools/subset_font.py`,
  which is the provenance of that modification, and CI fails when the committed
  subset and the current text disagree.
- The rig/component audits under `art/character_rig_audit/` are **historical structural
  evidence**, not licences or authorship declarations. The protagonist audit describes the
  **old** `assets/models/protagonist/protagonist.glb` (now the seated student), not
  `Protagonist_Character.glb`. The old Q-only audits, unrigged source, rig workfile and
  development duplicate have been retired with the former production Q. The current
  60-joint `new-Q.glb` is a separately approved ChatGPT-generated original, not a rigged
  derivative of that former asset; [Q_MODEL_MIGRATION.md](Q_MODEL_MIGRATION.md) records
  the comparison. The current runtime manifest explicitly covers all four production
  GLBs (old protagonist, new protagonist, new Q, N); `tools/model_library.py --check`
  compares structure, not legal status. This Q-only approval does not change the
  protagonist/portrait findings or close Issue #64.
- Substitute teacher N is recorded as Owner-authored in PR #130, including four
  five-second Blender loops. The game plays them,
  together, as N's idle (`CharacterAnimation.adopt_authored_layers()`); playing all four as
  layers of one idle is this repository's reading of four loops on the same key times,
  each moving a different part of the body, and the owner can change it in
  `scenes/environment/characters/teacher_n.tscn` (`authored_idle_layers`). Walking uses the
  procedural clips like every other character. The owner's export is kept unmodified under
  `art/character_models/teacher-n/` and the shipped file is rebuilt from it by
  `tools/prepare_teacher_n.py`, which needs `numpy` and `meshoptimizer` (see the script's
  docstring); a re-export replaces the source, its hash in the script, and the output.
- The project lead's hand-drawing/ChatGPT statement confirms part of the portrait pipeline,
  not the service terms or the rights of later replacements. Review the current source

## Rules

- Record the exact asset or pack and the individual files included.
- Keep a copy/link to the license version that applied when obtained.
- Check commercial use, redistribution, modification, attribution, and AI-training restrictions where relevant.
- Marketplace purchase does not automatically permit redistribution of source files.
- An AI-generated asset is not automatically cleared for commercial use; record the tool, applicable terms, prompt/source references where appropriate, and the human editing/approval process.
- Do not commit credentials, private user data, or assets with unclear rights.
